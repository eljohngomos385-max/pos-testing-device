Stock history · Gaps and ladders

Gap line: the old number and the new number are two ticks on one line, the gap between them shaded red (short / dearer) or green (over / cheaper). Ladder: one column per item, a rung for every 1 % it moved, old price grey at the foot, new price at the top rung.
