How this page decides things

  • Left is fixed: four KPIs, the hourly chart, recent transactions. Same order in every store, so staff always know where to look.
  • Right is the owner's: Edit widgets → ↑ ↓ to reorder, × to remove, Add widget at the bottom. The rail is one card wide, so widgets only ever stack and can't misalign.
  • Targets live in the rail and are always today and this month. The ··· on a target card opens the form: set the month, and the daily target works itself out.
  • On by default: targets, low stock, payment methods. Off by default: deliveries, stock value, sales by channel, credit used.
