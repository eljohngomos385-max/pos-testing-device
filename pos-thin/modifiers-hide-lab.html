Hide a choice on one item

Brown Sugar Milk Tea is already sweet, so it can't be made 0%. Every other drink keeps the full Sweetness list. You don't make a second Sweetness: you hide 0% on that one drink. Below: how you hide it, what the POS shows, and how you find it again with 100 items.

Unavailable

Out right now (no Pearls today). One switch on the choice, greyed on every drink that asks it. You turn it back on when it's in.

Hidden

This drink never offers it (Brown Sugar can't be 0%). Set once, on the drink. Not greyed: it just isn't there. Other drinks still show it.

The rules, for whoever builds it

Hidden

  • Hidden is on the link between a drink and a modifier: "Brown Sugar Milk Tea asks Sweetness, without 0%". The modifier and its choices don't change.
  • Set on the drink: Item › Modifiers › tap the modifier's name › a switch per choice, all on to start.
  • The drink's Modifiers page shows "4 of 5 choices" under a modifier that hides any; "All 5 choices" otherwise.
  • On the POS, a hidden choice is not drawn at all (not greyed). Unavailable is still greyed, on every drink.
  • At least one choice stays on: the last switch can't be turned off (to drop the question, deselect the modifier).
  • A new choice added later (say 60%) shows on every drink. Hide it where it doesn't belong.
  • Removing a choice also removes it from every "Hidden on" list.

Finding it

  • The modifier page shows "Hidden on N items" under any choice hidden somewhere.
  • Edit choice gets a Hidden on row (only when N > 0): the drinks, each with the same switch. Turning one on = shown on that drink again.
  • Modifiers tab search matches modifier names and choice names; the match is darker in the line under the name.
  • Not made: a ready-made list per drink, a report of all hidden choices. Add if 100+ items turn out to need it.
