Frequently asked questions

Do I need internet to sell?

No. The POS keeps selling offline and syncs your sales when you're back online.

What do I need to start?

A tablet, phone or computer with a browser. A receipt printer and a cash drawer are optional.

What kind of store is it for?

Hardware, grocery, cafés, bakeries and more. It only shows the parts your business needs.

Can I run more than one store?

Yes. Every POS in every store syncs into one back office.

Can my staff use it without training?

Yes. Each cashier signs in with a PIN and only sees what their role allows.

Can I bring my items from another POS?

Yes. Import a CSV in the back office and your items, prices and stock come with it.
