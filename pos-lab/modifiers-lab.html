Modifiers & add-ons, step by step

Everything a Gong cha store would set up for a Matcha Latte, done as real jobs. First the two words, then one scenario per job: what you want, every tap to set it up, and the POS screen you get at the end.

First, the two words

Modifier

A question about the drink

The POS asks it every time you sell the drink. The answer is written under the drink, on the same line. It is not a new item.

Each modifier is a set of choices. Example: Sweetness
0%25%50%75%100%
More examples

On the receipt: indented under the drink.

Add-on

Another item, offered with the drink

Something you already sell, like a snack. If the customer wants it, it goes on the order as its own line, with its own price, and it comes off its own stock.

Put them in an add-on set. Example: Snacks
Egg pudding ₱45Brown sugar cookie ₱35Apple pie ₱55
Or pick one just for a drink
Mango pudding ₱45
Matcha Latte Large185.00
Egg pudding45.00

On the receipt: a line of its own.

The quick test: is it a choice about the drink (how sweet, how big)? It's a modifier. Is it a different thing the customer also buys? It's an add-on.

Scenario 5

Selling it: the whole sale, start to receipt

A customer orders a large Matcha Latte, 50% sugar, less ice, pearls, and an Egg pudding. One question per screen. The modifiers come first, then the add-ons last. Try the playable one (step 2).

  1. Tap the drink.
  2. Answer each modifier question.
  3. Pick add-ons, tap Add.
  4. Check out: the cart and receipt show which is which.
