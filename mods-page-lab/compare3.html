10 ways to do the same 4 jobs: open a modifier, change the order, add, remove. Each one keeps a tap to one job. 50 modifiers in the store. Lab: /mods-page-lab/?mv=i … ?mv=r.

I · Three tabs

On this item = tap opens. Order = drag rows. All = tick on / off (all 50, A–Z, search).

J · Swipe and hold (like iPhone lists)

Tap opens. Swipe left = red Remove. Hold a row a moment, then move it.

K · ⋯ menu on each row

Tap the name = open. Tap ⋯ = Move up, Move down, Remove. No dragging.

L · Order on its own page

The list only opens. "Change order ›" is its own page where rows drag. Remove sits at the bottom of a modifier's page.

M · The number is the place

1 · 2 · 3 = when it is asked. Tap the number to pick a new place. Tap the name to open.

N · Opens in place

Tap a row: it opens right there with its choices and Move up · Move down · Remove. One open at a time.

O · Pop-up card

Tap a row: a card in the middle with its choices, Move, Remove, Done.

P · Tap in the order you want

One page lists all 50. Tap them in the order they're asked: each gets its number. Tap again to take it off.

Q · Pick, then edit below

Tap a row to pick it. Its choices, Move and Remove sit in the card under the list.

R · Select, then act

Tap opens. "Select" puts circles on the rows; the bar under them moves or removes what's picked.
