  • Fulfilment, payment and status are pills — the app's pill set, unchanged. Green done / cash, blue digital / delivery, amber in progress / credit, red voided, grey refunded / pickup. The word is always there, so nothing is colour alone.
  • The empty state keeps the headers so you can see what would be there, and uses the empty block's line.

Buttons

Two shapes, both 28px tall. Rectangles do things; pill buttons choose or filter things.

  • Dropdown: one white menu, hairline, 8px corners, 28px rows. As a filter (categories, suppliers, sort) the trigger shows the choice and the choice is ticked; behind a button (More actions) it's a list of actions, with a line before the risky one. Arrow keys, Enter and Esc work; clicking outside closes it.
  • Rectangle for actions. White with a hairline by default; soft grey for quiet ones beside it; one primary per view. Hover and press are two steps darker; nothing grows or moves.
  • Pill button for choosing: filters, ranges, a status you click to narrow the table. Same fills as the table pills, 28px instead of 20, and the chosen one goes dark.
  • Both are 28px, the same as the toolbar's search and dropdowns, so a row of mixed controls lines up.
