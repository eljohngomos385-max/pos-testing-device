Ask for choices: Nothing, Reference no., Last 4 digits, Approval code, Cheque no., Account no., Name, Phone no.

1. Payment types page

Every type except Split now opens its own page. The bin at the top right goes (see 3).

2. A type's page

Two rows become the same four cards on every type. Name shows on your own types only. Must fill in sits right under Ask for, and hides when Ask for is Nothing.

3. Deleting your own type (Visa)

Now: on the Payment types page you tap the bin at the top right, tick Visa, then tap the red bar. After: the bin at the top right is gone. You open Visa and tap the red "Delete payment type" at the bottom. Cash, GCash, QR, Other and Account have no delete.

4. Ask for

Now it's one switch that always asks "Reference no.". After, you tap Ask for and pick the word.

5. Checkout: GCash with a picture

With a QR picture added, the customer scans first, then Done.

6. Checkout: the box

It asks your word. With Must fill in on, Complete sale stays grey until it's filled.

7. Checkout: Cash

Same as today: tap the amount, sale done. The last picture only shows for a store that wants a number written down for cash.

8. Checkout: Split

Each part asks what its type asks, and follows Must fill in.

9. Receipt

Prints the word you picked under the payment.

10. Shift: no change

Expected cash stays cash only. A cheque already has its own line under "By method".

11. Deleting your own fulfilment type (Lalamove)

The same move as 3. Now: on the Fulfilment types page you tap the bin at the top right, tick Lalamove, then tap the red bar. After: the bin is gone. Lalamove gets a ›; you open it and tap the red "Delete fulfilment type". Walk-in, Pickup, Delivery, Dine-in and Take-out have no page and no delete.

12. Cash drawer

Now: nothing opens the drawer. After: a new Test drawer row under Test print. Tap Open and the drawer pops (it opens through the Wi-Fi or Bluetooth printer it's plugged into). After a sale, it pops when the payment type has Opens cash drawer on, then the receipt prints. No printer picked: it says so.
